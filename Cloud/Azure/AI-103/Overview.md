**Azure AI App and Agent Developer Associate Certification**


> *Compared to [[AZ-200]], this will be more focused on functional requirements / design of ai-powered services in Azure*
> 	[link to course](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/courses/ai-103t00) (available on 3/31/26)
> 		[link to (retired) AI-102 training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-engineer/?practice-assessment-type=certification)


- Will be replacing `AI-102: Azure AI Engineer Associate:`
	- **Retirement Date:** AI-102 will retire on **June 30, 2026**, with the final day to take the exam being that same date.
	- **Replacement Exam (AI-103):** The new exam, _Developing AI Apps and Agents (AI-103)_, is scheduled to enter beta in **April 2026** and go live in **June 2026**.
	- **What is Changing:** The focus is shifting from simply consuming Azure AI services (AI-102) to building, orchestrating, and developing production-ready AI agents and generative apps, specifically utilizing Microsoft Foundry.
	- **What to do if preparing for AI-102:** If you have already registered, you can still take the AI-102 exam. However, if you have not registered, it is strongly recommended to wait and prepare for the new AI-103 exam. 

> "**The Microsoft Certified: Azure AI App and Agent Developer Associate Certification** aligns with today’s generative and agentic architectures for planning and managing AI resources in Microsoft Foundry, building generative apps and multistep reasoning workflows, and developing production‑ready agents with multi‑agent orchestration capabilities. **Exam AI-103** beta available in April 2026; training expected to be available in March 2026; exam expected to go live in June 2026."
