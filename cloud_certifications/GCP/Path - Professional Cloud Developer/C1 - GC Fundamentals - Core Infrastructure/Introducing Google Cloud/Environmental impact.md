
# Google Cloud Environmental Responsibility

The virtual world, including Google Cloud's network, is built on physical infrastructure. Data centers globally use roughly 2% of the world's electricity, making energy efficiency a critical concern.

## Environmental certifications

Google's data centers were the first to achieve **ISO 14001 certification** — a standard that maps out a framework for an organization to enhance its environmental performance through improving resource efficiency and reducing waste.

## Case study: Hamina, Finland

Google's data center in Hamina, Finland is one of the most advanced and efficient in the fleet. Its cooling system uses sea water from the Bay of Finland to reduce energy use — the first of its kind anywhere in the world.

## Carbon and energy milestones

| Decade | Milestone |
